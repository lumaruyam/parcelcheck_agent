# ParcelCheck

Report a parcel that arrived damaged and get a decision in about a minute.

**How it works**
1. Click **📦 File a claim** and answer six short questions with the buttons: what was in the parcel, its value, the cover, the label fee, when it arrived, and a photo (or a short description).
2. Check the summary and click **Send**.
3. You get the decision, the refund rule it is based on, and the amount.

**Who decides**
- Refunds of €20 or less, when every check passes, are paid on the spot.
- Larger refunds, every refusal and unclear photos go to a person, within 24 hours.
- The amount always comes from the refund rules, never from the AI alone.

**Just looking?** Click **✨ Try an example** to see four typical claims.

Type **person** at any time to talk to a human. This service uses an AI system (EU AI Act, art. 50).
